Your lights, in your pocket.

NodeLight turns your phone — iPhone or Android — into a small lighting desk. It sends DMX over Art-Net to a wireless node, so you can set colors and levels, save looks as presets and run effects from anywhere in the room.

Read the manual Get support

Lights and sections

Fader and color for every light, organised by room or stage side.

Groups

Control many lights with one fader and one color.

Presets with fades

Record looks and recall them with timed fades in five styles.

Effects

Blink, Pulse, Chase, Sparkle and Rainbow, layered and synced to BPM.

Finds your node

Searches the Wi-Fi for Art-Net nodes and switches automatically.

Several phones

Phones on the same network share the live state — iPhone and Android together.

What you need

A phone with NodeLight, an Art-Net node, and DMX lights wired to that node.

Pknight Art-Net WiFi

The node NodeLight was made and tested with, in every demo. It makes its own Wi-Fi (the node answers at 192.168.4.1) or joins yours, and NodeLight finds it on its own.

See Pknight's nodes →

Any other Art-Net node

NodeLight sends standard Art-Net, so nodes from ENTTEC, DMXking and others work too. NodeLight finds them on the Wi-Fi the same way.

DMX lights

Any DMX light: RGB pars, dimmers, LED bars. Add each one with its universe, start address and type, or your own channels.

The Pknight link is an affiliate link: if you buy through it, NodeLight gets a small commission, at no extra cost to you.

Free to try

The free version controls one light, so you can test NodeLight with your own Art-Net node. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited lights and saving and opening shows. No subscription, no account, no ads.
